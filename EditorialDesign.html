Editorial Design

Overview

A collection of editorial design projects created in Adobe InDesign, exploring magazine layout, typography, visual hierarchy, image composition, and cohesive page design. The collection includes selected spreads from a Seven Wonders of the World magazine concept and two National Geographic-inspired editorial pages.

My Role

As the designer, I developed the layouts, typography, visual hierarchy, and overall composition for each piece using Adobe InDesign. For the Seven Wonders of the World magazine project, I focused on creating visually engaging spreads using provided written content; the writing and article text were not created by me. The National Geographic-inspired pages were created as editorial design exercises using placeholder Lorem Ipsum text.

Goals

The goal of these projects was to create visually compelling and cohesive editorial layouts while practicing principles of typography, composition, spacing, and visual hierarchy. I focused on balancing text and imagery to create layouts that were engaging, readable, and appropriate for a magazine or publication format.

Seven Wonders of the World Magazine

This project explored the design of a magazine focused on the Seven Wonders of the World. I created a cover and selected interior page spreads, developing the visual layout, typography, image placement, and overall composition in Adobe InDesign. The project includes only a selection of pages rather than the complete seven-wonder magazine. The written content shown in the spreads was provided for the project and was not written by me.
