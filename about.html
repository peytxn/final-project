About Me

Hi, I'm Peyton Blanchard, a designer and developer who enjoys creating intuitive and engaging digital experiences. I recently graduated from Michigan State University with dual B.A. degrees in Information Science and Games and Interactive Media, where I combined problem-solving, visual design, and storytelling across web and interactive projects. I also have a strong background in graphic design, which influences my approach to layout, typography, and creating visually clear experiences. Additionally, I minored in Computer Science, which has strengthened my understanding of programming and development principles.
